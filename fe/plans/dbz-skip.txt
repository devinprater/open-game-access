# DBZ AotS — drive the cutscene to its END with DENSE, LONG B holds.
#
# Two verified facts drove this plan:
#   1. the story boxes prompt with **B**, not A (read off a 3x panel);
#   2. a 10-frame press does not register — 30-60 frames does. The emulator
#      samples once per frame, so the press must span frames where the game
#      is ready to consume it.
#
# So: B held 40 frames, every 200 frames, for the whole run. That is ~5x
# denser than the plan that advanced only a few beats in 26000 frames.
