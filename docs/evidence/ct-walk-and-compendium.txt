READ THIS CAREFULLY. 0117=10 is $0117 bit 4 - the DOOR bump latch, raised by C0/9D4A
(ORA #10) with the tile stashed at $015B. It fired exactly once in 80 polls, on the
step that walked into tile 0x0713, and was consumed without effect: the consumer
C0/0D3E dispatches only on metatiles E6/EC/EE/FA/FC and the metatile at
$7E3000+0x0713 is not one of them. $0117 bit 7 (0x80) - the MAP EXIT pending bit,
raised at C0/9D24 and tested at C0/9CA1 - never appeared at all.
