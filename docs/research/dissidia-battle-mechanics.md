# Dissidia battle mechanics (manual + FAQ research, 2008 original)

Sources: GameFAQs FAQ 55224 + walkthrough 57707 (both quote the
manual), GameSpy/IGN guides, Dissidia wiki EX page. Verified where
noted against our host captures; visual-only claims marked (V).

## Controls (battle)

- Analog stick: move. D-pad: camera.
- X: jump (most chars double-jump), aerial recovery when launched,
  Chase confirm when prompted, also Confirm in menus.
- Circle: Bravery attacks (3 ground + 3 aerial, customizable).
- Square: HP attacks (slower; damage = current Bravery).
- Triangle: Map Action / Quickmove. Context-only: works while the
  yellow target marker shows. Wall runs, rail grinds, dash-jumps
  between platforms. No marker = no effect (probed twice, host).
- L: camera focus cycle: Free Camera, Lock-On opponent, Lock-On EX
  Core (Core lock needs the Lv2 EX Core Lock-On ability).
- R (hold): block/guard, front only. Timed block staggers the
  attacker: brief free counter. Some attacks crush guard.
- R+X: dodge, directional with analog (forward dodge slips behind).
- R+Circle: manual summon (non-Auto summons only, costs stock).
- R+Square: EX Mode when the gauge is full.
- R+Triangle: air-dash variants (need Action abilities equipped).
- SELECT: reset camera. START: pause.

## The Bravery system

- Bravery = your damage number. Brave attacks steal the foe's
  Bravery and add it to yours. HP attacks deal your current Bravery
  as damage, then your Bravery resets low.
- Bravery Break: drive their Bravery to zero and you score bonus
  stage Bravery. Fights are few decisive hits, not long combos:
  careful strikes beat button mashing (interrupted combos lose the
  best final hit).
- HUD bottom: portraits, HP bars, big Bravery numbers (we read
  1000/1000 WoL vs 1368/1368 Firion live, host).

## EX system

- EX Force: light-blue orb clusters shed on damage. Walk through
  them to fill the vertical purple EX Gauge.
- EX Core: glowing bell that spawns near the higher-LUK fighter;
  wings show value (1/2/4 = some/a lot/full). Touch it for a big
  fill. Untouched cores absorb ambient Force and grow (up to 2
  upgrades, intangible while transforming). "EX Core in play!"
  banner seen live (host). L can lock them with the ability.
- EX Mode (R+Square at full gauge, turns orange): per-character
  bonuses, +50% crit, HP Regen for ~20s while the gauge drains.
  Activation breaks enemy combos.
- EX Burst: land an HP attack in EX Mode, hit Square at the prompt:
  signature cinematic minigame (Simon-style/mash). Perfect input =
  full damage; fail = weak finisher. Ends EX Mode. Seen live as
  "THE FINAL BLOW!" flash + "DEFEATED..." KO card (host).

## Chase, walls, summons

- Chase: launching hits trigger a slow-mo aerial clash. Timing game:
  dodge on their turn to steal a strike, X to keep chasing. Ends at
  a wall/obstacle slam or after 3 dodges.
- Wall Rush: slammed into walls/stage furniture for bonus damage.
- Summons: R+Circle manual or Auto types; counter-summon timing
  matters. Effects are big visual + audio events (future beacon?).

## Accessibility notes

- Combat timing is heavily visual: Chase prompts, dodge windows, EX
  Burst minigames, yellow Quickmove markers, wall/void awareness.
  None have known audio cues; hit sounds + BGM exist but unmapped.
- Lock-on reticle is visual-only: our centered-audio beacon has no
  in-game equivalent to piggyback on; it must be adapter-side.
- Story-map enemy piece looks encode strength: bare helm =
  Minimal-Low(Actions), helm+sword = Low(Equipment)-Average, gold =
  to High(Actions), armored = to High(Equipment), spiked rare =
  to Maximum. Matches our 9 Quick Battle strengths: readable proxy.
- Cosmos/Chaos Battle Rules (PP Catalog, post-game) change fights
  globally (gauge fills, freeze for passivity): reader must know
  which rule is active.
